Many people try to define what poetry is by the structure of its content (e.g., whether it rhymes or not and its meter and length and number of stanzas). Others by the way it makes a reader feel. Poetry, for me, is something that grants access to the world in the high-dimensional terms with which I deal with it. In this case, its structure and the way it makes others feel is secondary. It is how it makes me feel. The structure and content is just a means to feeling awe and wonder.
