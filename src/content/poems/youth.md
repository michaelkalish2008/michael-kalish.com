---
title: Petals of Youth
date: June 2026
note: Original from Nov 2007 | Inspired by Dylan Thomas
order: 1
id: petals-of-youth
---

In the trellised air of my youth,
I hiked the high trail of perfume,
Under a salt work of stars,
Caring nothing for the patchwork of the elbowed towns,
Or the moth light of the city streets,
My stomach lowed like cattle,
I was king of the table,
Crowned with a head of hair.

Braids of incense filled my nose,
I was greener than ivy,
Happy with the colors of dawn,
Carefree in the routine’s irons,
My mind drained in its mould,
And my rindless heart bathed all grains of salt,
Gay beneath the canopy of grapevines,
Bud breaking as a newborn star.

And as time lingers in the nose,
So it ran through my horse drawn fingers,
With the summer petals that she loves me,
And heads of pennies and dust trails of rabbit,
Under the fiery shadow of the east borne blossom,
There joy bloomed on the saddle of my desire,
My heart shackled by chains of bees,
I sang on the throne of my appetite,
Round as apples,
In a body of port and bread.

It was harvest,
The long and short hand governed pay,
As summer blew its dry whistle,
And the rivers begged for rain.
My hunger pulled the reins,
Bound to the bounty of town,
Over the wheels of my pleasure
Skirting the dresses of the cherry voiced wind.
The sky flower warmed the way
And as the bees stored the pollen of the land
I filled the jar of my chest with the sea.

So down the wick and wax of light,
Riding the shadow of its dials
I nursed on the utters of my lot
Into the fleeing colors of age.
Fall shook its golden head.
The bees balled like yarn,
And the butcher hung his bloody shanks.
In town the gavel of words created order
Shadows of great trees grew taller
Crowns of once proud stems line the road,
Faded gold over petals of frosted hope.

Now under the wing-borne pollen of the earth drawn flower
Through the hoops and staves of night,
Fall the apples of my song
And the sexless chambers of their seeds
Long sowed with kingly joy into the valleys of rain.
And in the clockwork of my nose,
Over the wheels of my eyes
The paste and rind of my heart aged firm
Beneath the apple high moon
In the petals that she loves me.
